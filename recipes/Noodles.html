Chili Crisp Cold Noodles

Credit: Chef John

“These chili crisp cold noodles are the perfect hot weather dish. Extraordinarily flavorful, they come together very quickly and can be used as a base for sliced meats, served over lettuce, or enjoyed as they are.”

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup unsweetened smooth peanut butter
  • 1/3 cup soy sauce
  • 1/3 cup Chinese black vinegar (or balsamic vinegar)
  • 2 tablespoons rice wine vinegar
  • 3 cloves garlic, crushed
  • 2 teaspoons grated fresh ginger
  • 2 teaspoons white sugar
  • 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil
  • 1/3 cup hot noodle water

Steps

  1. Cook noodles according to package instructions.
  2. Reserve some hot noodle water.
  3. In a bowl, whisk peanut butter with soy sauce and vinegars.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, sugar, and sesame oil.
  5. Slowly mix in hot noodle water until smooth.
  6. Toss noodles in the sauce until fully coated.
  7. Serve chilled or at room temperature.
