Classic Lasagna

Credit: Allrecipes – World's Best Lasagna

This classic lasagna is layered with rich meat sauce, creamy ricotta, melted mozzarella, and tender pasta sheets. It’s comforting, hearty, and perfect for family dinners.

Ingredients

  • 12 lasagna noodles
  • 1 pound ground beef
  • 1 pound Italian sausage
  • 1 onion, chopped
  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 jar (24 oz) marinara sauce
  • 2 cups ricotta cheese
  • 1 egg
  • 2 cups shredded mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • Salt and pepper to taste
  • Fresh basil (optional)

Steps

  1. Boil lasagna noodles until al dente. Drain and set aside.
  2. Cook ground beef, sausage, onion, and garlic until browned.
  3. Stir in marinara sauce and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Mix ricotta, egg, salt, and pepper in a bowl.
  5. Spread a thin layer of meat sauce in a baking dish.
  6. Add a layer of noodles, ricotta mixture, mozzarella, and sauce.
  7. Repeat layers until ingredients are used up.
  8. Top with mozzarella and Parmesan.
  9. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 40 minutes.
  10. Let rest for 10 minutes before slicing.
